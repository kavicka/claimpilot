# Interview guide: travel claims handler (30 minutes)

Goal: learn how a travel claim really moves today, where time goes, and which rules live only in
people's heads. Ask about the last real claim, not about claims in general.

## Warm-up (3 min)
1. What do you handle: which products, which claim types, roughly how many claims a week?
2. What does a good day look like? A bad one?

## Walk through the last claim you closed (10 min)
3. How did it arrive (portal, email, post, broker)? In what shape: scans, photos, originals?
4. What did you open first? What did you check before anything else?
5. Which systems did you touch, in what order? Where did you retype data?
6. Where did you wait: on the client, on a doctor, on a colleague, on a system?
7. How did you decide how much to pay? What did you look up, and where?

## Exceptions and tacit rules (8 min)
8. Which claims do you hand to a senior colleague, and why those?
9. What makes you suspicious? Give me the last example.
10. Which rules are not written anywhere but everyone follows?
11. Which documents are hardest to read (language, handwriting, scan quality)?
12. How do you handle foreign currency and which exchange rate do you use?

## Measurement (5 min)
13. How is your work measured? Who looks at those numbers?
14. Which mistakes are expensive: paying too much, paying too little, paying too late?
15. How often does a claim come back (complaint, reopened, audit finding)?

## Close (4 min)
16. If a tool prepared each claim before you opened it, what would it have to show for you to trust it?
17. What would you never let a tool decide?
18. Who else should I talk to?
